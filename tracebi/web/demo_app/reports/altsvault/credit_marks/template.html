The headline: marks belong to the debt book

Splitting the portfolio by instrument class is not cosmetic — it is what makes a mark meaningful. The debt book marks at — of cost across — positions. The equity and warrant book appears to mark far above cost, but that is an artifact of nominal cost bases: a warrant booked at a token cost and now carried at millions reports a mark in the thousands of times cost. Those positions inflate any ratio they sit in, which is why every mark statistic below is taken on the debt book.

Where fair value sits by mark band

UNCOMPUTABLE is not a stress category — it is the — coverage gap seen from the other side: positions with no usable cost basis, where a mark cannot be computed at all rather than being computed as bad. At the extreme, — funds carrying — report no computable mark on any position.

How these numbers were made

Phase ① is ordinary pandas and is not machine-checked. What is checked is the sink: the declared contract checks ran as read-only SQL when the tables landed, including an exact reconciliation of every position's fair value to its fund's book value. The claim is "the sink satisfied its contract" — never "the transform was verified". What the pipeline states about itself follows.
