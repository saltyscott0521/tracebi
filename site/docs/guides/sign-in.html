Sign in with your company login

Turn on single sign-on so people sign in to TraceBi with their company account and get a role from their groups. The CLI uses the same sign-in through tracebi login.

Before you begin

  • An OIDC identity provider (Okta, Entra ID, Google Workspace, Keycloak)
  • The connector setup from Connect ChatGPT and Claude, which registers the app with your provider

For admins: turn it on

  1. Set these variables on the server: TRACEBI_OIDC_ISSUER, TRACEBI_OIDC_CLIENT_ID, TRACEBI_OIDC_CLIENT_SECRET, TRACEBI_OIDC_ROLE_MAP and TRACEBI_PUBLIC_URL. The app's sign-in turns on with them.
  2. At your identity provider, add a second redirect URI to the same app registration: https://bi.example.com/login/callback. The first, https://bi.example.com/oauth/callback, is the connectors'.
  3. Map your admins' group in TRACEBI_OIDC_ROLE_MAP before you restart. Roles apply from the first request.
  4. Restart. The log prints app sign-in: /login.

What changes

  • Roles are enforced. viewer reads and queries. analyst also runs reports and writes drafts. admin also runs pipelines and keeps selection cuts. A person in no mapped group gets TRACEBI_OIDC_DEFAULT_ROLE (viewer). Without sign-in, enforcement is off so a running server is never shut out of its own pipelines; turning sign-in on is your choice to enforce.
  • It replaces Basic and proxy-header auth. The server refuses to start with TRACEBI_OIDC_ISSUER and either TRACEBI_AUTH_USER/TRACEBI_AUTH_PASS or TRACEBI_AUTH_PROXY_HEADER.
  • A proxy in front must let some paths through. If a proxy has a login of its own, let /login, /logout, /login/callback, /authorize, /token, /register, /revoke, /oauth/callback, /mcp and /.well-known/ through without it.

For users: sign in to the app

  1. Open the app. Without a session you are sent to the company login, then back to the page you asked for.
  2. The sidebar shows the places your role uses: Reports and Docs for everyone; Review and Data for analysts and admins. Only an admin runs a refresh or sets a credential.
  3. To sign out, click Sign out in the sidebar footer. This ends the TraceBi session. Your identity provider may still be signed in, so the next visit can sign you back in without a password.

For users: sign in from the command line

tracebi login --server https://bi.example.com

This opens your browser at the company login and stores the result in ~/.config/tracebi/credentials.json ($XDG_CONFIG_HOME is respected), readable only by you. The address must be https, or loopback for local testing.

tracebi logout --server https://bi.example.com

This revokes the sign-in at the server and removes the tokens from the file.

Tools that call the API for you send Authorization: Bearer <token> and act as you, with your role. tracebi.credentials.access_token(server) returns a valid token and refreshes it when needed. When the sign-in has ended it tells you to run tracebi login again.

What happened

  • The session is a random id in a cookie (tracebi_session: HttpOnly, Secure over https, SameSite=Lax). The database stores only its SHA-256. It ends after 12 hours unused, 7 days in all, or when you sign out.
  • Your role is read at sign-in. A change of group applies at the next sign-in.
  • Protected paths. The page shell (/, /assets) loads without a session because it holds no data. Every /api, /r/ and /dashboards/ request needs one: the API answers 401 and the app sends you to sign in. /api/health stays open for health checks.
  • Who am I. GET /api/me returns {actor, role, sign_in}, where sign_in is oidc, basic, proxy or none.
  • CLI tokens are TraceBi access tokens that last one hour, with a refresh token that rotates on each use (7 days sliding, 30 days at most). The CLI receives them on a one-time listener on 127.0.0.1 (a random port), and registers itself with the server once.

If it does not work

You see Likely cause
"Sign-in could not be verified" after the company login Wrong client secret or issuer, or …/login/callback is not registered at the provider. The server log has the reason.
"Started in a different browser" The sign-in link was opened in a different browser from the one that started it. Start again.
A 403 for something you could do before Your group is not mapped to a role that allows it.
tracebi login: "says its address is …" TRACEBI_PUBLIC_URL on the server differs from the address you passed.
tracebi login waits and the browser shows a client error The server lost its record of this CLI. Run it again; it registers afresh.
