Sources

  • Mortgage rate. Freddie Mac, Primary Mortgage Market Survey: the thirty-year fixed-rate average, weekly, averaged to each calendar year. Retrieved through FRED, Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis.
  • Median home price. U.S. Census Bureau and HUD, median sales price of houses sold: quarterly, averaged to each calendar year. Retrieved through FRED.
  • Median household income. U.S. Census Bureau, median household income (CPS ASEC, table H-8), current dollars. Later years are also on FRED as median household income in the United States.
  • Computed here. Monthly payment, share of income and price-to-income are worked out from the three series above in transforms/affordability_transform.py.

The series in this report is a snapshot typed in from the published tables, not a live pull. Refresh it from the sources with python inputs/fetch_housing.py before citing a figure. See inputs/housing_sources.md.

How these numbers were made

Rates are Freddie Mac's weekly survey averaged over each year. Prices are the Census and HUD median sales price of houses sold. Income is the Census median household income, in the dollars of its own year.
