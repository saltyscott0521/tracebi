How these numbers were made

Rates are Freddie Mac's weekly survey averaged over each year. Prices are the Census and HUD median sales price of houses sold. Income is the Census median household income, in the dollars of its own year. See inputs/housing_sources.md, and refresh the series with python inputs/fetch_housing.py before citing it.
