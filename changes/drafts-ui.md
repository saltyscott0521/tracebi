### Added

- **Drafts** in the sidebar, beside Verify a file. `/drafts` lists the reports and models being written before they are published, newest first, each marked "Changed" or "Same as published". A draft's page shows the report rendered live (it follows edits to the draft's files without a reload), its files read-only, and **Publish** (with an optional note, after a confirm step) and **Delete**. A draft that does not render, or a publish that is refused, shows the reason inline.

### Changed

- A report frame with nothing in it to operate is no longer a keyboard stop.
